# Inoma Digital – Email Signatures

Three HTML email signature variations for Inoma Digital team members. Uses theme blue `#0277b5` and works on both light and dark email backgrounds.

## Variations

1. **signature-1-compact.html** – Compact horizontal: logo left, details right, blue left border
2. **signature-2-centered.html** – Centered stacked with bordered card
3. **signature-3-minimal.html** – Minimal with blue divider line

## Placeholders to replace

- `[YOUR_LOGO_URL]` – Full URL to your hosted logo image (e.g. `https://yoursite.com/logo.png`)
- `[Your Name]` – Full name
- `[Your Title]` – Role (e.g. Founder, Creative Director)
- `[your.email@inomadigital.com]` – Email address
- `[+92-XXX-XXXXXXX]` – Phone number

## How to use

1. Host your logo image somewhere (CDN, your domain, etc.).
2. Open the chosen `.html` file, replace all placeholders including `[YOUR_LOGO_URL]`.
3. Copy the full HTML and paste into your email client’s signature settings.
