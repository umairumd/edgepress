Frequently Asked Questions

SEO is the process of optimizing websites to improve visibility in organic search and attract relevant traffic.
SEO increases online visibility, drives qualified traffic, builds credibility, and supports long-term digital marketing growth.
SEO results vary, but most websites see noticeable improvements within 3–6 months, depending on competition and strategy.
The main types of SEO are on-page SEO, off-page SEO, technical SEO, and local SEO.
